# DevAtHome User Guide

Welcome to DevAtHome! This guide will help you get started and make the most of your experience.

## 1. Creating an Account (Sign Up)
- Go to the Sign Up page.
- Fill in your personal details (username, first name, last name, email, date of birth).
- Choose a strong password (at least 12 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character).
- Select two different secret questions and provide answers. These will be used for password recovery.
- Submit the form to create your account.

## 2. Logging In
- Go to the Login page.
- Enter your email and password.
- If you enter the wrong password three times in a row, your IP will be blocked for 10 minutes for security.

## 3. Resetting Your Password
- Click "Forgot password?" on the login page.
- Enter your email address.
- Answer your two secret questions.
- If your answers are correct, you can set a new password (must meet the strong password requirements).
- You will see a confirmation and can return to the login page.

## 4. Uploading and Managing Photos
- After logging in, go to your profile or the feed.
- Use the upload button to add new photos.
- You can view, like, and comment on photos.
- You can delete your own photos from your profile.

## 5. Security Features
- Passwords must be strong (12+ chars, uppercase, lowercase, number, special char).
- After 3 failed login attempts from the same IP, login is blocked for 10 minutes.
- All login attempts are logged for security.

## 6. Contacting Support
If you encounter issues or need help, contact the DevAtHome support team at: support@devathome.com
